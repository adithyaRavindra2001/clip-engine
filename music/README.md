# Background music beds

Drop royalty-free tracks here and the engine mixes one quietly under every clip, ducked under the speech.
Claude tags each clip with a mood; a track is picked from the matching folder (any folder if that one is empty).

    music/energetic/   fast, driving, for hype moments and big claims
    music/tense/       low pulses, suspense, for confrontations and hard questions
    music/funny/       light, bouncy
    music/emotional/   soft piano / pads, for reveals and personal stories
    music/neutral/     anything that works under talking

Where to get tracks you are allowed to burn into uploads:
- YouTube Audio Library (studio.youtube.com > Audio library): free, no attribution needed for most, safe on YouTube and Instagram.
- Pixabay Music, Uppbeat (free tier): check the licence line for "no attribution" and social-media use.
Do NOT use trending TikTok/Reels sounds or any commercial song here: Content ID will claim or block the upload,
and clipping campaigns reject claimed clips.

MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, OGG and FLAC all work. 5-10 tracks per folder is plenty.
